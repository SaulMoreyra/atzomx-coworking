## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the thoughtful intersection of artisanal specialty coffee culture, organic community spaces, and contemporary digital nomad workflows. It balances tactile warmth with architectural precision, avoiding overly corporate sterility while maintaining the utility needed for booking, remote work sessions, and community interaction.

The visual style merges **Warm Organic Minimalism** with **Editorial Typewriter Nostalgia**:
- **Generous negative space** grounded by natural earth tones: matcha wash, oat cream, and deep forest olive.
- **Craft and character** through monospace display typography paired with legible, geometric body text, celebrating deliberate communication and analog roots.
- **Accents of delicate soft lavender** that introduce an unconventional, creative hospitality feel without overpowering the botanic calmness.
- **Tactile structure** built with crisp hairline borders, pill-like interactive elements, and line-drawn architectural iconography.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from botanical ingredients, unbleached paper, and specialty café interior finishes.

### Roles & Application

- **Primary (`#283618` - Deep Forest Olive):** Used for primary action buttons, key typographic headlines, deep interactive accents, and structural outlines. Conveys solidity, permanence, and calm authority.
- **Secondary (`#C7DBC5` - Soft Matcha Sage):** Serves as an environmental container tint, hero background alternate, badge backdrops, and active-state pill highlights. Evokes tea, foliage, and relaxed focus.
- **Tertiary (`#D8B4E2` - Pastel Wisteria Lavender):** Used selectively for secondary interactive triggers (e.g., top navigation menus, specialty tags, seasonal badges), adding a gentle artistic contrast against green and cream fields.
- **Neutral Surface Base (`#FBF9F1` - Warm Oat Cream):** The predominant background layer across the experience. It reduces glare compared to pure white, feeling like recycled bond paper or linen.
- **Neutral Tone Variant (`#F5F3E8` - Parchment):** Used for nested cards, secondary panels, and zebra striping.
- **Text Primary (`#1F2E1E` - Charcoal Forest):** High-contrast readable dark olive-black for continuous prose and inputs.
- **Text Muted (`#5F6D56` - Dried Sage):** Subdued label color for inactive pricing tiers, list dash items, and meta timestamps.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs an expressive, retro-technical monospaced face (`Space Mono`) with an inviting, modern grotesque (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in `Space Mono` uppercase or generous tracked casing. Evokes typewriter-stamped café menus, remote telegraph dispatches, and code terminals.
- **Body & Long Prose:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` for maximum readability across long session bookings, community guidelines, and blog entries.
- **Labels, Pills & Metadata:** Always set in uppercase `Space Mono` with extended letter-spacing (`0.12em` to `0.15em`) to retain the feel of stamped utility tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system designed around airiness and vertical breathing room:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `container-max: 1200px`, centered with `2rem` gutters. Section padding ranges between `4.5rem` and `6rem` vertically, encouraging a slow, editorial browsing tempo.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters. Cards reflow from two-column asymmetric arrangements to single-column or balanced double grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` edge margins. Touch targets maintain minimum 48px heights with stacked pricing tables and full-bleed hero cards.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an intentional 8px base rhythm with 4px half-steps for compact monospace labels and tag insets.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system intentionally rejects heavy, synthetic drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering and Hairline Outlines**.

1. **Surface Tiers:**
   - **Base Floor:** `#FBF9F1` (Warm Oat Cream) or `#C7DBC5` (Sectional Matcha blocks).
   - **Raised Panels & Cards:** `#FBF9F1` surrounded by a `1px` solid outline in `#283618` (Forest Olive) or `#C7DBC5`.
   - **Interactive Floating Nav / Sticky Triggers:** Subtle border overlay with `rgba(40, 54, 24, 0.12)`.
2. **Ambient Elevation:**
   - When elevation is physically necessary (e.g., hover states on pricing cards, dropdown popovers), use a soft, natural green-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(40, 54, 24, 0.08)`.
   - No directional multi-drop shadows or neon glows are permitted.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects deliberate duality:
- **Buttons, badges, and status tags** embrace soft, friendly curvature (`0.5rem` / `rounded-md` up to full `9999px` pills for schedule status badges).
- **Cards, feature sheets, and imagery containers** maintain crisp architectural corners (`0.25rem` to `0.5rem`) or framed architectural borders, balancing the softness of café life with the structural discipline of a modern coworking venue.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background in `#283618` (Forest Olive), text in `#FBF9F1`, typography in `button-text` (uppercase monospace), `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px). Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover shifts to `#1F2E1E` with a slight 1px inset transform.
- **Secondary / Accent Button:** Background in `#D8B4E2` (Soft Lavender), text in `#1F2E1E`, hairline border in `#283618` or borderless pill. Used for actions like "MENÚ" or special notifications.
- **Outline / Pill Trigger:** Background transparent, `1px` border in `#283618`, text in `#283618`, fully rounded capsule pill (`rounded-full`), padded `0.35rem 1.25rem` for tags like "HORARIO".

### Cards & Plan Containers
- Outlined with a continuous crisp `1px` solid border (`#283618`).
- Inner padding of `2rem` on desktop, `1.25rem` on mobile.
- Background set to neutral canvas (`#FBF9F1`).
- Included price badges: Pill container with `#C7DBC5` (Matcha) fill and dark olive monospace bold digits.

### Lists & Feature Comparison
- **Included Perks:** Preceded by a custom drawn hairline checkmark icon (`✓`) in `#283618`.
- **Excluded Perks:** Preceded by a thin em-dash (`—`) in muted dried sage (`#5F6D56`), communicating subtle status without aggressive red warnings.
- Space between items: `0.85rem` with generous line height for effortless scanning.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Background in white or neutral canvas `#FFFFFF`.
- Border `1px` solid `#283618` with `0.375rem` radius.
- Labels rendered in `label-mono` above the input in `#283618`.
- Focus state: `2px` solid `#283618` with no external neon glow.

### Badges & Language Selectors
- Language Switcher: Compact pill or outlined box with icon (`🌐`), uppercase monospace label (`ES`), and subtle caret. Border `1px` solid `#283618`.
- Status Tags: Soft matcha or lilac pills with `0.25rem 0.75rem` padding, communicating open hours, wifi strength, or remote desk availability.
